## 7. Honest testimonial

Any testimonial given by the Owners reflects their own honest opinions, beliefs, and experiences. AB Development will not change the meaning of a testimonial, and will not publish it in a way that is misleading. The Owners may give a testimonial that is positive, negative, or mixed; this Release is not conditioned on a favorable review. *(This section reflects the FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising and its rule on consumer reviews and testimonials. ## 8. Privacy protections

AB Development will take reasonable care not to publish:

- House numbers, street signs, license plates, or other details that identify the exact address, unless the Owners agree in writing.
- Security systems, cameras, alarm panels, safes, locks, or access codes.
- Personal documents, mail, family photos, or screens showing personal information.

- [ ] **Additional request:** Do not publish interior images that show valuables, artwork, or personal belongings.

AB Development may edit, crop, or blur images to meet these protections.

## 9. Duration and revocation

This Release continues until revoked. The Owners may revoke it at any time by notice **in writing** to AB Development at [email address] or [mailing address]. Revocation:

- Applies **going forward only**. It does not affect uses made before AB Development received the notice.
- Gives AB Development a **reasonable time** (about [30 days]) to remove the Materials from its website and from social media accounts it controls. AB Development cannot control copies made or shared by others.
- Does **not** require AB Development to recall or destroy **printed materials** already produced or distributed, although AB Development will not reprint them with the Materials.

## 10. Ownership

All photographs, video, and recordings made by or for AB Development under this Release are owned by AB Development (or the photographer who licenses them to AB Development). The Owners do not acquire any ownership, copyright, or license rights in them. Written testimonials remain the Owners' own words, and the Owners grant AB Development a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use them as described in this Release.
